# CVPR 2026 Poster Spec

Verified on `2026-05-13` against official CVPR 2026 pages:

- Poster printing: `https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2026/PosterPrinting`
- Author guidelines: `https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuidelines`
- Main conference poster and video uploads: `https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2026/YouTubeVideoPosterUpload`
- Shared templates and artwork source provided by the conference or organizer: `https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1oaXlMOJzWMYUiFBImMepKsZcoicpks8Z`

## Official shared assets

When available, export the needed files from the shared asset folder and use them as follows:

- templates or slide decks: place under `references/`
- logos and conference marks: place under `assets/logos/`
- extra conference labels such as session or acronym assets: keep alongside other reference materials

## Main Conference and Findings

- Final size: `84in x 42in`
- Metric size: about `213cm x 107cm`
- Orientation: `landscape`
- Aspect ratio: `2:1`
- Recommended layout: `3` or `4` columns
- Export: `PDF` preferred
- Resolution guidance: `100 DPI` at full size or vector art
- Bleed: `do not include bleed`

## Workshop

- Final size: `42in x 21in`
- Orientation: `landscape`
- Aspect ratio: `2:1`
- Export: `PDF` preferred
- Resolution guidance: `100 DPI` at full size or vector art
- Bleed: `do not include bleed`

## Official guidance to reflect in the poster

- Use little text
- Use a few large expressive figures
- Do not make the poster a copy-paste of the paper
- The poster should support a `5` to `10` minute conversation
