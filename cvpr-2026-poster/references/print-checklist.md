# Print Checklist

Use this checklist before poster export and printing submission.

## Poster content

- The title matches the camera-ready title.
- The author list and affiliations are final.
- The paper ID is shown correctly.
- The poster supports a live explanation in `5` to `10` minutes.
- Text is sparse enough to scan from a distance.
- The core method figure is large and readable.

## Technical export

- The track matches the final size.
- The poster is landscape.
- The final export is `PDF`.
- No bleed is included.
- Raster images are high enough quality for large-format print.
- Thin lines, legends, and captions are readable when zoomed to print size.

## Conference submission

- The print job name uses the required format.
- The earliest required pickup date is noted.
- The poster printing deadline has not passed.
- If this is a main conference paper, the poster page upload requirements are tracked.

## Final sanity check

- QR code opens the intended final destination.
- Figures are not stretched.
- No anonymous wording or placeholder text remains.
- Session-specific logos or labels are correct.
